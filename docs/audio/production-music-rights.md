# Production music rights and acquisition record

Acquired on 2026-08-09 from the composer's official Incompetech download
endpoints for Poker Training Pro. The user explicitly authorized local download
and inclusion of tracks whose redistribution license was verified.

All ten tracks are composed by Kevin MacLeod. Each official track page names
the track and ISRC and provides the CC BY 4.0 attribution code. Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 permits commercial sharing and adaptation with credit.
The official legal code is bundled at `licenses/audio/CC-BY-4.0.txt`.

Required in-app credit:

> Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons
> Attribution 4.0 International.

Individual titles, ISRCs, and source-page links are shown in the application's
Credits screen and recorded in `src/data/musicPlaylistManifest.ts`. The app
does not provide standalone extraction, does not stream the files, and must not
register these tracks with Content ID.

The official Incompetech FAQ identifies an in-game credits screen as the normal
attribution route for games and permits editing, compression, and splicing:
https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html
